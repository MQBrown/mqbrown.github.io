About

I’m Melbourne Q. Brown, a senior software engineer and technical leader who designs and builds secure, scalable systems for real businesses—across energy, insurance, and consulting.

I’ve led projects at Southern Company, GEICO, and McKinsey, working across .NET, Azure, event-driven architecture, and AI agents. I care about systems that are not just elegant on paper, but maintainable, observable, and aligned with business outcomes.

Outside pure tech, I’m building MQBrown Homes, a Georgia-based real estate and construction venture, where I apply engineering thinking to builder economics, licensing, and operations.
